Welcome to the World of Spanish Speaking Countries! Hurry and dive in to explore unique traditions, rich culture, and delicious food!

There are 21 Spanish speaking countries in the world. Many people may think that all Spanish countries are the same but they are very mistaken. Each country has its own unique traditions, history, food, music, and culture! Within this website students will be able to research and learn all about the differences that each country brings to the world. You’ll be able to dive deeper in your learning by watching short videos, looking at pictures, drooling at delicious recipes, listening to music, and more! I hope that through your research you’ll be able to share with others not only what you’ve learned but how it’s changed your perspective of the world!
